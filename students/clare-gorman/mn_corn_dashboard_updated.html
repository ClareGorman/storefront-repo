Where the data comes from, where it is stored, how it is filtered

  • Comes from: USDA NASS Quick Stats, downloaded as a CSV (see the date in the comment at the top of this file).
  • Stored: the CSV text is pasted into this file in the hidden block csv-data, with a second copy in mn_corn_production.csv. No internet connection is needed.
  • Filtered: the CSV has final annual numbers (Period = "YEAR") and monthly forecasts (Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov). The bar chart keeps only Period = "YEAR". The forecast table shows the others for the selected year.
